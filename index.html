Official Privacy Policy for MilkTooth: Baby Teeth Tracker

This privacy policy applies to the MilkTooth: Baby Teeth Tracker app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Muammer Dev (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information locally on your device to track your baby's teeth growth (such as baby name, birth date, gender, and tooth photos). None of this personal baby data is uploaded to external servers; it remains securely on your device.

However, the Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device or analyze performance. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA for iOS or AAID for Android) used for advertising and attribution
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
  • The operating system and hardware model of your mobile device

Third Party Access & Data Processors

The Application uses reliable third-party services to display advertisements, process premium subscriptions, and improve app functionality. Below are the links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the Application:

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

While the Application is designed to help parents track their babies' teeth development, the Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. All data entry must be performed by a parent or legal guardian. If you are a parent and discover that your child has directly provided personal information to us without your consent, please contact us at muammerdev3@gmail.com so we can take immediate corrective actions.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain user-provided local data as long as the Application remains installed on your device. Uninstalling the Application will permanently erase all local records, including photos and baby profiles, unless backed up via device-wide system cloud backups.

Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. Therefore, we strive to protect your data. Since all baby profile data and photos are stored strictly in local device storage, the overall security depends heavily on your device's security configurations.

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will become effective immediately upon being published on this page.
