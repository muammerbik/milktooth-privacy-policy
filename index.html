Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Policy for MilkTooth: Baby Teeth Tracker

This privacy policy applies to the MilkTooth: Baby Teeth Tracker app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Muammer Dev (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information locally on your device to help parents track their baby's teeth development (such as baby name, birth date, gender, and tooth photos). This personal baby data is not uploaded to the Service Provider's servers; it remains on your device unless you use device-wide cloud backups (e.g. iCloud).

Advertising & Tracking

The Application does not display advertisements and does not track you:

  • No cross-app tracking — the Application does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • No advertising identifiers — the Application does not collect or use the iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or the Android Advertising ID (AAID). Advertising ID collection and ad-personalization signals are explicitly disabled in our analytics configuration.
  • No data selling — we do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the Application is used and to improve it, we use Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects anonymous, pseudonymous usage information, such as:

  • App interactions — for example which screens and features are used (e.g. a tooth was added, a brushing session was completed, the premium screen was opened or a plan was purchased), without any content you enter
  • A random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase (not linked to your name, email or phone number)
  • Device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate country/region (derived from IP address; precise location is not collected)

Analytics never receives your baby's name, birth date, photos, notes, tooth records or any other content you enter. This data is used only for product analytics; it is not used for advertising or for tracking you across apps or websites. You can reset the identifier at any time by reinstalling the Application.

Purchases & Subscriptions

Premium plans (monthly and yearly subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase) are processed by the Apple App Store (iOS), Google Play (Android) or Huawei AppGallery, and managed via RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription status information linked to an anonymous app user identifier in order to validate your purchase and restore it. We never receive your payment card details.

Third Party Access & Data Processors

The Application uses the following third-party services to process purchases and improve the app. Their privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Google Play Services
  • RevenueCat (In-App Purchases & Subscriptions)

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

While the Application is designed to help parents track their babies' teeth development, the Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information directly from children under 13 years of age. All data entry must be performed by a parent or legal guardian. If you are a parent and discover that your child has directly provided personal information to us without your consent, please contact us at muammerdev3@gmail.com so we can take immediate corrective actions.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain user-provided local data as long as the Application remains installed on your device. Uninstalling the Application will permanently erase all local records, including photos and baby profiles, unless backed up via device-wide system cloud backups.

Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. Since baby profile data and photos are stored in local device storage, overall security depends heavily on your device's security settings (passcode, biometrics, and system updates).

Changes to this Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will become effective immediately upon being published on this page.
